<p>(Last Updated: September 29, 2026)</p>

<p>I am a master’s student in Machine Learning at <a href="https://mbzuai.ac.ae/">MBZUAI</a>, where I am advised by <a href="https://lahlou.org/">Prof. Salem Lahlou</a>, <a href="https://nilslukas.github.io/">Prof. Nils Lukas</a>, and <a href="https://www.contrib.andrew.cmu.edu/~kunz1/">Prof. Kun Zhang</a>. I am currently a visiting researcher at <a href="https://www.cmu.edu/">Carnegie Mellon University</a>, where I work with <a href="https://mireshghallah.github.io/">Prof. Niloofar Mireshghallah</a>.</p>

<p style="margin-bottom:0.5em">My research aims to build AI systems that remain trustworthy as they become more capable and autonomous. To achieve this goal, I focus on four fundamental research questions:</p>

<p style="margin:0 0 0.35em 0">(i) <b>Robustness:</b> How can we develop AI systems that perform reliably across diverse and unforeseen conditions?</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 0.35em 0">(ii) <b>Security:</b> How can we understand and mitigate vulnerabilities that adversaries with bounded capabilities can exploit in AI systems?</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 0.35em 0">(iii) <b>Privacy:</b> How can we quantify and limit sensitive information leakage from AI systems?</p>
<p style="margin:0 0 0.35em 0">(iv) <b>Safety:</b> How can we understand and mitigate risks of harm associated with AI systems?</p>

<p style="margin-top:1em">Previously, I worked on explainable cyberbullying detection and multimodal content moderation. I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering from <a href="https://www.iitp.ac.in/">IIT Patna</a>, where I was fortunate to be advised by <a href="https://www.iitp.ac.in/~sriparna/">Prof. Sriparna Saha</a> and <a href="https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~pb/">Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya</a>. My undergraduate thesis received the Best Bachelor’s Thesis Award at IIT Patna. I was also a research assistant at the <a href="https://www.sutd.edu.sg/">Singapore University of Technology and Design</a>, where I worked with <a href="https://info.roylee.sg/">Prof. Roy Lee</a>.</p>
